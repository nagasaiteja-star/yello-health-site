The problem

The top line is flat. The margin is squeezed from both sides.

Scanners sit idle for part of every day while fixed costs keep running, and aggregators and hospital labs set the price. Owners don't need more equipment. They need patients, and someone to run the floor.

The model

We run the centre. The owner keeps it.

Under a fully managed partnership Yello does the heavy lifting: demand, operations, clinical systems, staff, tech and brand. The owner keeps full ownership and a guaranteed floor. We never buy machines — capital goes into filling centres, not building them.

One centre

₹34.5 lakh a month becomes ₹87 lakh.

The owner's scanner doesn't change; the calendar does. Our demand lifts a centre by half in its first year, and keeps compounding to maturity. The room has the workings.
