For investors

An operator's plan for independent diagnostics.

Independent diagnostic centres in India have good machines, capable doctors, and flat top lines. Yello runs them: we bring the demand, the operations and one standard, and the owner keeps the centre. NDIAN Healthcare Private Limited is the company. Yello is the name patients see.

The problem

Owners have capacity, not demand.

Equipment sits idle for part of the day, fixed costs don't move, and aggregators and hospital labs set the price. Revenue stalls and the margin gets squeezed from both sides.

What we do

We run the centre. We don't buy the machines.

Under a fully managed partnership, Yello runs the centre end to end. The owner keeps a guaranteed monthly floor, a share of the growth and full ownership. Our spend goes into onboarding, fit-out and ramp, not scanners.

What we're proving

One real centre, measurably grown on our demand.

Before we scale, we're showing that one partner centre's monthly revenue rises on the patients we bring. The investor room holds the plan, the numbers and how we'll measure it.

Who's building it

Led by doctors.

Dr. Sandeep Juvvadi, Co-founder & CEO, is a radiologist who built and led radiology at Tenet Diagnostics, Hyderabad. Dr. Naga Sai Teja Gadde is Co-founder.
