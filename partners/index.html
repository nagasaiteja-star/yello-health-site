Common questions

Partner FAQ.

Ownership & control
Do I keep ownership of my centre?

Yes. In Fully Managed and Branded Partner you keep 100% ownership, premises, equipment and capex — Yello operates or supports it, never takes it over. We never require you to sell.

Money
How does Yello make money — what do I pay?

By model: Fully Managed — Yello runs the centre and earns from the growth it creates; you keep a guaranteed monthly payout set against your current revenue, plus rent, and a share of that growth. Branded Partner (opens in Phase 2) — a brand & platform licence plus a share only on the bookings Yello brings you. You never pay a demand fee on your own patients. Exact terms are shared one-to-one after the discovery visit.

What does it cost to join?

₹0 onboarding fee for the pilot cohort, and no upfront payment from you. You keep your own capex; in turnarounds we can bring capital, structured separately.

What if my centre is loss-making right now?

That's exactly who Fully Managed is built for. We bring brand, demand, network procurement and operating discipline to turn it around — and we're paid mainly when performance improves.

What you get
How do you bring me patients?

Digital marketing, doctor engagement, corporate tie-ups, idle-slot discounts (Yello Hours), and a patient app that keeps families coming back — legal, transparent platform demand with no referral kickbacks.

What happens to my staff?

We train and retain your team, give them SOPs and career paths, and reduce attrition. We're not a layoff machine — strong centres run on good people.

Compliance & exit
Is the demand legal? What about referral commissions?

Yes. The NMC bars doctors from taking referral commissions — that grey economy is a licence risk. Yello demand is platform demand: transparent routing and marketing, not a kickback.

How long is the contract, and can I exit?

The term and exit terms are agreed up front in your operating agreement, before anything changes at your centre. We also offer planned exits later — a planned buyout, or help with a strategic sale.
