1. What this test is

NIPS looks for common chromosome changes in a fetus using a blood sample from the mother. It is a screening test. A screen-positive result needs confirmation by a diagnostic test chosen by your clinician. A screen-negative result lowers, but does not remove, the chance of a chromosome condition.

2. Results and turnaround

Results go to the ordering clinician, who will explain them. Turnaround times are estimates and can change with the complexity of the test or the quality of the sample. The report does not diagnose a condition or recommend treatment.

3. Sample quality

If a sample is inadequate or fails quality control, Yello Diagnostics may ask for a repeat sample. Samples damaged in collection or transport by anyone other than Yello staff are the responsibility of the collecting party.

4. Fees and refunds

Fees are exclusive of taxes. [Refund window and payment terms to be added.]

5. Legal compliance

Testing is carried out under the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (PCPNDT) Act, 1994. The sex of the fetus is not disclosed.

6. Your data and sample

Your personal and health information is handled under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the Yello privacy notice on yello.health. Your sample is kept for the laboratory retention period and then destroyed. You may withdraw consent at any time; samples not yet used will be destroyed. Any use of your anonymised data for research needs a separate, optional consent.
