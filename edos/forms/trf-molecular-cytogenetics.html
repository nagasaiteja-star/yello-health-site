Genetic testing consenthere "I" means the patient or guardian

I ask Yello Diagnostics to carry out cytogenetic / molecular testing on my sample (amniotic fluid, chorionic villi, fetal cord blood, peripheral blood or products of conception) to find out whether my fetus or I have an increased chance of the condition named as the reason for referral. Where this test is done on an invasive prenatal sample, my doctor has given me a separate consent (Form G) covering the risks of that procedure.

I understand that

  1. The test needs enough good-quality cells or DNA. Yello Diagnostics is not responsible for problems caused by improper shipment or wrong submission of samples.
  2. Sometimes the sample is not enough to reach a conclusion, and the test may need repeating.
  3. More tests, or samples from the affected person, parents or other relatives, may be needed to give a clear answer. These may be needed before a prenatal sample is accepted.
  4. If the family relationships are not as stated, the interpretation can be wrong.
  5. Genetic tests keep improving. Very rarely a test may not work properly or an error may occur.
  6. Leftover sample is not returned. It is kept under the laboratory retention policy. Extra studies on it can be done only on a request from the referring clinician, and may be charged.
  7. My sample will not be used for validation or research without my separate consent.
  8. I can withdraw consent at any time; any unused sample will be destroyed promptly.
  9. Reports go to the ordering clinician only. Results are confidential and are not released to other professionals without my written consent.
  10. Results may be reported as normal, abnormal, inconclusive, or as a test failure.
  11. For a prenatal sample, results apply to this pregnancy only. Only a doctor can explain the chance of recurrence.

My signature below shows that I take part in this test willingly.
